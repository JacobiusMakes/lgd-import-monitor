Subject: New monthly series: official US imports of cut lab-grown diamonds (they fell 88% in one month)

Hi <name>,

Nobody publishes the US import series for cut lab-grown diamonds on a monthly basis, so we
started. The Lab-Grown Import Monitor is a fixed-format release built from Census (HTS
7104.91.10.00) and cross-checked against UN Comtrade, with the data, the scripts, and the
methodology open under CC BY.

What edition one shows. US imports of cut lab-grown diamonds averaged $88.9 million a month in
2024. They fell from $52.4 million in August 2025 to $6.5 million in September 2025, an 88 percent
drop in a single month, and have averaged $13.7 million a month since, about 15 percent of the
2024 pace. First half of 2026: $107.2 million, down 74 percent from the first half of 2025 and 81
percent from 2024. [Update with July if published: latest month $X, up or down Y percent year over
year.]

Two things the data does not do. It does not move to other origins: India supplied about nine
dollars in ten in 2024, its share dipped into the sixties in late 2025 while the UAE rose to one to
two million dollars a month, and it was back above ninety percent by spring 2026. And it is not a
reclassification: the whole HS 7104 heading fell the same way and the neighboring lines stayed
flat.

The timing lines up with the India tariff going to 25 percent on 7 August 2025 and 50 percent on
27 August, the February 2026 reduction, and the Section 301 regime at 10 percent from 24 July.
We report the coincidence and do not assert the cause; the release says so in those words.

One more number that has never been public: the Census count runs between 0.4 and 2.9 million
stones a month at an average declared value of $8 to $27 per stone, so by count this line is
melee. The value series is the one that tracks finished-jewelry supply.
